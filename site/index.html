Scepter of Goth

Havenford

A river city with four roads out of it: a graveyard, a keep of locks and traps, a broken observatory and the wilds to the north. A multi-user role-playing game you play in a MUD client.

How to play See the map

Server sceptish.pbbgaming.com, port 4000

How to play

  1. Install Mudlet

    Mudlet is a free MUD client for Windows, macOS and Linux.

  2. Add the game

    In Mudlet's connection window, make a new profile:

    Server
    sceptish.pbbgaming.com
    Port
    4000
    Secure
    off

  3. Make a character

    Connect and type a new name. The game asks for a class, attributes, a weapon skill, a password and a sex.

    You start in the Havenford Refuge with 200 shillings. Buy a weapon and armour at the Provisioner before you leave the city.

The map

Every room in Havenford, with its exits, signs and monsters. A route finder gives you the moves from one room to another.

Good to know

  • The connection isn't encrypted. Your password crosses the network in plain text, so don't use one you use anywhere else.
  • Characters are saved at second level. Before that, a character is lost when you leave.
  • Havenford is a beta test world. The game may be restarted or reset while it's being tested.
  • Any MUD client works, such as TinTin++ or MUSHclient, and so does plain telnet sceptish.pbbgaming.com 4000.
